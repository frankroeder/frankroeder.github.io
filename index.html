Dissertation

Language Grounding in Deep Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic Goal-Oriented Robotics

Frank Röder

Doctoral Thesis, Hamburg University of Technology (TUHH), 2026

Reviewers: Prof. Dr. rer. nat. Nihat Ay, Prof. Dr. Pierre-Alexandre Murena

DOI RL Algorithms Robotics Simulation

Language is the primary medium for instructing embodied agents, yet robots struggle to ground natural language robustly under conversational noise such as disfluencies and polysemy. This thesis studies these limitations in a sparse-reward, language-conditioned reinforcement learning setup, using linguistic feedback and egocentric speech to learn from failure in hindsight — relabeling unintended deviations as alternative goals and predicting goal commands that match the observed behavior. A second pillar tackles action correction, resolving ambiguous or miscommunicated instructions through further verbal input and a context-sensitive hindsight method that models goal uncertainty.
